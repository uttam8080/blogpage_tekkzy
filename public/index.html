hello@happyprancer.com
What is Happy Prancer? +
Happy Prancer is an all-in-one circular wellness club blending daily yoga flows, Zumba cardio, and cultural dance workouts to build consistent movement habits.
What is our mission? +
Our mission is making healthy movement joyful, sustainable, and free from intimidating gym environments.
Do I have to pay any subscription charges? +
We offer a 7-day free trial on all memberships. You can pause or cancel anytime with zero cancellation penalties.
I am a GYM owner, how can I connect? +
We partner with verified studio spaces for hybrid classes. Reach out to partners@happyprancer.com with your studio location.
What is a hybrid class? +
A hybrid class gives you live stream access along with physical credits to attend partner studio sessions in person.
